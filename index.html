Your data

Everything lives in this browser. Export saves your shelf metadata, reading positions, bookmarks, and loans as JSON. Book files are not included; downloaded books re-fetch on import, and uploaded files need uploading again.

About

Shelfmark reads DRM-free EPUBs with epub.js, PDFs with pdf.js, and CBZ comics with JSZip. Search comes from Gutendex. Covers and metadata for loans come from Open Library. Suggestions run through your own recommendation endpoint at /api/recommend.
